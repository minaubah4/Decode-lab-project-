# Tech Troubleshooting

> Trigger: When the user reports a technical problem using the platform such as login issues, access problems, bugs, or errors.

# Steps
1. Ask the customer to describe the specific technical issue they are experiencing on the Decode Labs platform (e.g., login failure, broken page, document download error, or portal access issue).
2. Use {{label:Knowledge Search,id:search_knowledge,type:builtin}} to look up known platform issues, troubleshooting guides, or system statuses matching their description.
3. Guide the customer through standard self-service troubleshooting steps based on their specific platform issue:
   - If they are having login or portal access issues: Ask them to confirm they are using the exact email address registered during enrollment, try an Incognito/Private window, or clear their browser cache.
   - If they cannot download documents (Offer Letter, Certificate, LOR, or Invoice): Instruct them to verify their pop-up blocker is disabled and that they are downloading directly from their personal Access Portal.
   - If they encounter an error during project submission: Prompt them to verify that the project submission link they are pasting into the Project Tracker is publicly viewable.
4. If the troubleshooting steps do not resolve the issue, ask the customer to provide the following reproducible details:
   - The registered email address associated with their Decode Labs account.
   - The specific device, operating system, and browser they are using.
   - The exact error message shown or a description of what happens when the issue occurs.
5. Create a technical support ticket with the gathered details. *(No ticketing integration is connected yet; the agent will route these details to the engineering team manually).* {{todo:high:Connect a ticketing or CRM integration so technical issues can be logged automatically}}
6. Inform the customer that their technical issue has been documented and escalated to the technical team for review, and that updates will be sent to their registered email address. {{todo:medium:Confirm the expected response SLA for escalated platform technical issues}}
