# Smart Survey

> Trigger: When the user agrees to take a survey or you need structured answers to decide the best next step for them.

# Steps
1. Explain to the user that this quick survey helps Decode Labs recommend the best technical track and program structure for their career goals.
2. Ask the user which technical domain interest area they lean toward:
   - Software & Cloud (e.g., Full Stack Development, Java, Cloud Computing, Cyber Security)
   - AI & Data (e.g., Artificial Intelligence, Python, Data Science, Data Analytics)
   - Design & Business (e.g., UI/UX Design, Digital Marketing, Stock Market Trading)
   - Emerging Tech (e.g., IoT, Blockchain, Prompt Engineering)
3. Ask the user to select their primary goal for the virtual internship:
   - Get placement-ready fast
   - Build strong portfolio projects
   - Move into AI-focused or advanced technical roles
   - Work in business, design, or analytics roles
4. Ask the user about their weekly time commitment capacity:
   - 4–5 hours/week
   - 6–8 hours/week
   - 9+ hours/week
5. Analyze their answers to determine the best match:
   - If they are a prospective student or looking to enroll in a new internship program, guide them to the next step of qualification. {{label:Lead Qualification,id:lead-qualification,type:playbook}}
   - If they are an active intern needing technical guidance, project support, or specialized mentor feedback, route them to schedule or connect with a mentor. {{label:Mentor Session,id:mentor-session-request,type:playbook}}
   - If their needs are not covered by those options, search the knowledge base to recommend specific Decode Labs tracks matching their interests. {{label:Knowledge Search,id:search_knowledge,type:builtin}}
