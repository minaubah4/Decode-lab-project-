# Lead Qualification

> Trigger: When the user shows buying intent, asks about pricing for teams or organizations, or requests a demo or sales contact.

# Steps
1. Ask the customer to provide their preferred role or interest track (e.g., AI & Data, Software & Cloud, Design & Business, or Emerging Tech).
2. Ask about their primary goal for the virtual internship (such as becoming placement-ready, building a portfolio, or pivoting to a new technical role).
3. Ask for their preferred weekly commitment (4–5 hours, 6–8 hours, or 9+ hours) and when they want to start.
4. Ask for their organization size or team budget range if they are inquiring on behalf of a group or company. *(No booking or CRM tool is currently integrated; once connected, this qualification data will sync automatically.)* {{todo:high:Connect a CRM or sales-tracking integration to automatically log qualified leads}}
5. Collect their full name, email address, and WhatsApp contact number.
6. Summarize the details back to the user, and inform them that the team will reach out via email or WhatsApp within 24 business hours to finalize their custom cohort plan. {{todo:medium:Confirm the lead follow-up SLA, "24 business hours" was assumed}}
