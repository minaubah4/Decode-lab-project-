# Mentor Session

> Trigger: When the user asks to book, schedule, reschedule, or cancel a mentor session or office-hours check-in.

# Steps
1. Ask the customer to provide their registered email address and the specific topic or questions they want to discuss with their mentor.
2. Ask for their preferred dates, times (with timezones), and their preferred contact method (such as WhatsApp or Email). {{todo:high:Verify if mentor sessions are conducted via Zoom, Google Meet, or Discord, and what the standard scheduling lead time is}}
3. Inform the customer that their request has been logged and that our scheduling team or their assigned mentor will reach out within 24 business hours to finalize the session. {{todo:high:Confirm the exact SLA timeline for mentor scheduling responses, 24 business hours was assumed}}
4. Direct the customer to the Intern Portal on the Decode Labs website (decodelabs.tech) where they can also use the "Chat & Query" feature to message their assigned mentor directly for quicker doubt support.
