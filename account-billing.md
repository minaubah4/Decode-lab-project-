# Account Billing

> Trigger: When the user asks to update their account, manage billing, cancel a plan, or asks about refunds, invoices, or policies.

# Steps
1. Ask the customer to clarify their specific request:
   - If they want to download an invoice or verify a payment status: Direct them to log into the Access Portal at decodelabs.tech using their registered email, where they can download invoices directly from their student dashboard.
   - If they have an active billing dispute, payment failure, or request that cannot be resolved in the portal: Ask for their registered email address, name, and registered internship track.
   - If they are requesting a refund: Explain that refund eligibility depends on the specific cohort and promotional terms. {{todo:high:Confirm the official refund policy and eligibility window for Decode Labs internships}} Ask for their registered email and track.
   - If they want to cancel their internship program: Ask for their registered email, track, and their reason for cancellation. {{todo:medium:Confirm the cancellation policy and if any cancellation fee applies}}
2. Search the knowledge base using {{label:Knowledge Search,id:search_knowledge,type:builtin}} to check for specific, up-to-date cohort refund rules, payment methods, or regional pricing details if the customer asks detailed policy questions.
3. Once the customer's details (email, track, and specific request) are gathered, instruct them to send an official request to hr@decodelabs.tech (or decodelabs.tech@gmail.com) so the operations team can process the account update, cancellation, or billing adjustment. *(No billing management integration is currently connected; once an integration is active, this process will be automated).* {{todo:high:Connect a billing or CRM database integration to allow direct processing of cancellations and refunds}}
